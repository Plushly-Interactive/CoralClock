Storage pruning

BiteGuard tracks every domain and subpage you visit. Over time, brief visits and accidental clicks accumulate noise — short stays on pages you never meant to track. This tool finds and removes those.

A domain or subpage is considered insignificant when, summed across every record ever stored for it (daily and hourly buckets, within each selected store), both its total active time and total audio playback time fall below the threshold below. A site briefly opened once is caught; a site briefly opened many times that totals real time is kept.
